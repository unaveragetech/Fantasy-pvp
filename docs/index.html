What this is

A galactic sports league you run from your phone

You're the manager. Open mystery lockers to sign athletes, build squads for the sports they suit, and send them out against other managers' teams. Then watch the whole match play out in 3D and share the replay with anyone.

🔓

1. Sign athletes

Every locker hides someone new. Rarer pulls get more light, a signature pose and their own sound.

📋

2. Build your squads

A brilliant striker might be a terrible tennis player. Talent differs by sport, so every game type rewards a different roster.

⚔️

3. Compete

Play Ranked against other managers' teams, practise against bots, or challenge a friend by username.

🎬

4. Watch and share

Every match becomes a replay you can follow from any athlete's view, and you can send anyone a link to it.

Why it's better with more players

Every new manager makes the league bigger

You don't need a friend online at the same moment. Your saved team plays whenever someone takes it on, so the league keeps moving while you're away.

More rivals, more variety

Each manager brings a different roster and tactics, so more players means more teams to figure out.

Rankings that mean something

Only Ranked games between real managers count. Bot wins can't buy a place on the table, so climbing it is a real claim.

Replays worth sending

A last-second win is one link away from your group chat, and anyone with the link can watch it.

Bragging rights across 27 sports

You might rule football and get flattened at table tennis. There's always another title to go after.

What we're building

Where this is heading

This is an early beta, built in the open. Right now we're focused on making athletes, locker reveals and match sound as striking as we can. After that, the goal is a league that's busy all the time, on servers that don't depend on one computer.

Fair by design. Coins are earned by playing and can't be bought or cashed out, and rarity never changes match strength. Every contest records its odds and roll, so you can check how a match was decided. Beta note: the server can go offline, and data could be lost.
